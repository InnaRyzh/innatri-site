Will I have enough time?

What training with me looks like

  1. Intro call

    A free call about your goal, race date, experience, health and schedule. If you have watch data, I look at it beforehand.

  2. Plan in TrainingPeaks

    I build your first week. Every session shows the goal, duration, heart rate zones, pace or power.

  3. You train and write a few words

    Data from your watch and bike computer uploads automatically. After each session you leave a short comment on how you feel.

    I compare plan and reality, read your comments and change the next days when needed.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to train for an Ironman?

It is individual: it depends on your base in swimming, cycling and running, your health and your schedule. From zero it takes longer. I will give you an exact timeline after our intro call.

Can I train for an Ironman while working full time?

Yes. Most of my athletes train this way, and many of them are executives. I distribute the time you have and set the load you can recover from.

How many hours a week does Ironman 70.3 training take?

Usually 6–9 hours, more in peak weeks. For a full Ironman, 10–14 hours in peak weeks. I build the plan from your real schedule.

What should I do if I miss a session?

Do not automatically move it to the next day. One missed session rarely decides anything. If needed, I will rebuild the next days.

What if I have a business trip or a holiday?

Let me know in advance. We look at the conditions of the trip and decide which sessions to keep, replace or drop.

Can I train for a triathlon if swimming is my weak sport?

Yes. First I assess your level, then we work on technique and breathing with video, gradually adding volume and open water.

Do I need an expensive bike and a power meter?

Not for your first race. A road bike in good working order and a GPS watch with heart rate are enough. You can train by heart rate and feel. I will tell you what is worth buying as we go.

How much does an online triathlon coach cost?

Full coaching is 200 USD a month: the plan, session reviews, direct contact and race strategy. The BASE plan at 150 USD includes the plan and analysis of key sessions without messenger support.
