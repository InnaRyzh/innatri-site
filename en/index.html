The main problem for amateur athletes is trying to fit the unfittable. You are juggling Ironman or marathon preparation with business, family and flights.

Template plans do not work here - they only lead to overtraining and guilt over a broken schedule.

I solve this problem. My job is to take away the daily burden of figuring out how to distribute your training load correctly.

Online coaching advantages

Managing the entire preparation

All training is managed through TrainingPeaks. I analyse volume, intensity, heart rate, pace, power, recovery and your feedback. Based on this data, I adjust the programme and decide when the workload should be increased, maintained or reduced.

A plan built around your schedule

Work, travel or family commitments should not derail the entire preparation process. I rebuild the training week so that the key purpose of the phase is preserved without trying to compensate for every missed session.

Results without unnecessary volume

I do not use workload simply to create the feeling of hard work. We develop endurance, speed and efficiency gradually, taking into account your current level, training history and ability to recover.

Triathlon coaching FAQ

Who are your training programs for?

Anyone who wants results without injuries. From beginners who want to run their first 5K, to experienced athletes preparing for an Ironman.

What do I need to get started?

  • A watch with heart rate monitor and GPS
  • TrainingPeaks app installed
  • Desire to train and discipline

All other equipment (bike, trainer, wetsuit) is acquired gradually as needed and as your goals grow.

How quickly will I see results?

The first physiological changes are felt within 2-4 weeks of regular training. Steady growth in functional metrics comes after 8-12 weeks of systematic work. Triathlon is a sport for the patient.

How does working with you look?

We start with diagnostics and goals, then you receive a plan in TrainingPeaks. Every week I analyze your workload and adjust tasks to match your reality.

Can I combine this with work and family?

Yes. The plan adapts to your schedule. What matters isn't a perfect routine, but consistency and regularity.

Do I need to be fit to start?

No. We start from your current level and gradually build fitness without overloading the body.

How often are we in touch?

You can always write to me. The plan is adjusted as needed, and a key analysis is done every week.
