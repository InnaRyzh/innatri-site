What happens next

  1. Goal assessment

    We discuss your sporting background, current fitness, health, available training time, work schedule and chosen event. I assess whether the goal is realistic and recommend the most suitable coaching format.

  2. System setup

    We connect TrainingPeaks and your sports watch, define training zones, analyse your current data and build the first phase of the programme.

  3. Personal coaching

    You complete the sessions while I analyse the data, collect your feedback and adjust the plan regularly. Before race day, we also build a detailed strategy for pacing, nutrition and completing the distance.
