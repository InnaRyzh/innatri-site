Frequently asked questions

Do I need running experience to start with a coach?

No. I coach runners of every level, from complete beginners to experienced marathoners. Your plan starts from your current fitness.

How long does it take to train for a marathon?

It is very individual: it depends on your current fitness, health, schedule and goal. I will give you an honest timeline after our intro call and a fitness check. I explain how marathon training is built in Marathon training for beginners.

How long does it take to train for a half marathon?

That is individual too, and I will give you a timeline after a fitness check. The good news: three runs a week are enough for a half marathon.

How does online running coaching work?

Every week you get a plan in TrainingPeaks. Your watch syncs automatically, I review heart rate, pace and cadence, and adjust the next week.

What watch and apps do I need?

A GPS watch with heart rate (Garmin, Polar, COROS, Apple Watch) and the free TrainingPeaks app.
