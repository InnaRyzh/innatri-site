Privacy Policy

Updated: August 10, 2026

This website (innatri.com) belongs to Inna Ryzhykh, a running and triathlon coach. This page describes what data the site collects and what happens to it. Short and free of legal fog.

What I collect

  • The diagnostic form (quiz): your name, contact (Telegram or email), city, and your training answers — distance, race date, weekly volume, experience, injuries, goal.
  • Analytics: the site uses Google Analytics — anonymised visit statistics via cookies (which pages you opened, from which device). This data does not tell me who you are.

Why

Quiz data serves one purpose: to reply to you personally and prepare a review of your training. Analytics helps me understand which materials are useful and improve the site. No newsletters without your consent, no spam, no selling of data.

Where the data lives

Quiz submissions are stored in my private Google Sheet (a Google service) and reach me as a Telegram notification. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services act as technical data processors — only I have access to the content of submissions.

For how long

As long as needed to answer your request and for possible cooperation. On your request I will delete everything immediately.

Your rights

You can ask what data I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted. Message me on Telegram or WhatsApp — I will reply and act.

Cookies and analytics

You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's official add-on or your browser settings — the site works fully without cookies.

Tell me about your race, current shape and schedule - I'll answer honestly which format will get you there.
